What Size Tank Do African Cichlids Need?

African rift-lake cichlids (mbuna, peacocks, haps) are active and territorial. A common starting point is a 40 gallon breeder or 55 gallon for a managed mbuna colony, with many keepers preferring longer tanks so fish can claim territories along the length.

Rough starting sizes

TypeCommon minimum
Small mbuna colony~40 breeder / 55 gal
Peacocks / haps~75 gal+
Mixed Malawi community~75-125 gal

Overcrowding is sometimes used deliberately with mbuna to spread aggression, but only with strong filtration and careful species choice. Beginners should under-stock and add slowly.

Setup tips

  • Hard, alkaline water suits most rift-lake species; see the pH, KH and GH guide.
  • Plenty of rock caves reduces fighting.
  • Size filtration above a typical community tank (see the filter GPH guide).
