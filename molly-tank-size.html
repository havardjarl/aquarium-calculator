What Size Tank Do Mollies Need?

Common mollies (sailfin and short-fin) are active livebearers. A widely cited minimum is about 20 US gallons for a small group, with 29-40 gallons more comfortable once they breed. Sailfins push toward the larger end.

Rule-of-thumb sizes

SetupCommon minimum
Trio (1 male, 2 females)~20 gal
Small breeding group~29-40 gal
With other community fish~40 gal+

Keep more females than males to reduce harassment.

Care notes

  • Mollies generally prefer harder, more alkaline water; convert readings with the hardness converter.
  • They produce a fair amount of waste; size the filter generously (see filter flow).
  • Some keepers add a small amount of aquarium salt for certain mollies; check species needs with the salt calculator.
