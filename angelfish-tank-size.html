What Size Tank Do Angelfish Need?

Freshwater angelfish can reach around 6 inches long and 8 inches tall including fins. A common hobby recommendation is a tank of at least 29-30 US gallons for a single angelfish or a pair, and 55 gallons or more for a small group. Height matters: many keepers look for tanks at least about 18 inches tall.

Rule-of-thumb sizes

SetupCommon minimum
One or a pair~29-30 gal, tall
Group of 4-6 juveniles~55 gal
Group in a mixed community~75 gal or more

Groups often pair off and become territorial as they mature, so plan for extra space or rehoming.

Care notes

  • Angelfish are commonly kept around 76-82 F (24-28 C); size the heater with the heater guide.
  • They may eat very small fish such as adult neon tetras as they grow (see the neon tetra guide).
  • Gentle to moderate flow and tall plants or decor suit them.
