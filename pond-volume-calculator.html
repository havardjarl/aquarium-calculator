Pond Volume Calculator

One cubic foot holds about 7.48 US gallons. For a rectangle, volume is length x width x average depth; for a round pond it is pi x radius squared x average depth.

Irregular ponds

Most garden ponds have sloped sides and shelves, so measure depth in several places and use the average. For a kidney or free-form shape, many people measure the longest length and the average width and then knock off roughly 10-20% to allow for curves. The most accurate method is to read a water meter before and after filling.

Why volume matters

  • Pumps and filters are rated by pond volume and flow per hour; many koi keepers aim to turn the pond over at least once every 1-2 hours.
  • Water treatments and salt are dosed by volume (see the dechlorinator dose guide and the salt calculator).
  • Water weighs about 8.34 lb per gallon, which matters for raised or patio ponds (see the weight chart).
