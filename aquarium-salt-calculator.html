Aquarium Salt Dosage by Gallons

Important caveats

Aquarium salt (sodium chloride without additives) is used by some keepers for certain freshwater treatments. Many plants, scaleless fish, and invertebrates dislike salt. Never use table salt with iodine or anti-caking agents unless a vet or product label says otherwise.

Always follow a medication or veterinary protocol when treating disease. Use real water volume from the volume calculator (after substrate), not the tank's nominal label.
