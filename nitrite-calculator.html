Nitrite After a Water Change

This models simple dilution. It assumes the new water mixes fully and that bacteria are not instantly removing nitrite during the change.

Why nitrite matters

Nitrite is toxic to fish. It often appears in the middle of a cycle when ammonia-oxidizing bacteria are active but nitrite-oxidizers have not caught up yet. See the fishless cycling guide.

Tips

  • Test with a liquid kit; strip tests can miss low readings.
  • Large water changes lower nitrite but do not finish the cycle by themselves.
  • Pair with the ammonia dilution tool and nitrate calculator.
