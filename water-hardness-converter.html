Aquarium Water Hardness Converter

Test kits report hardness either in German degrees (dGH for general hardness, dKH for carbonate hardness) or in ppm, meaning milligrams per liter expressed as calcium carbonate. The conversion is the same for both: 1 degree = 17.848 ppm. Many test kit instructions round this to 17.9.

Which number matters

GH is mostly calcium and magnesium and matters for fish, shrimp and snail health. KH buffers pH and keeps it from crashing. The pH, KH and GH guide explains how they fit together, and the CO2 calculator uses KH to estimate dissolved CO2.
