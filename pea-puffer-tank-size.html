What Size Tank Do Pea Puffers Need?

Pea puffers (dwarf puffers) are tiny, about 1 inch as adults, but territorial. A widely repeated hobby guideline is about 5 US gallons for one pea puffer, plus roughly 3 gallons for each additional puffer. Dense plants and decor matter as much as gallons because they break line of sight and cut down on nipping.

Rule-of-thumb stocking

TankPea puffers
5 gal1
10 gal~2-3
20 gal~5-6, heavily planted

Many keepers suggest more females than males, or a species-only tank, since pea puffers often nip other fish.

Care notes

  • They are commonly kept around 74-80 F (23-27 C); size a heater with the heater guide.
  • Most refuse flakes and are fed frozen or live foods; small snails are often offered as well.
  • They are sensitive to ammonia and nitrite, so add them only to a fully cycled tank (see the cycling guide and the ammonia calculator).
