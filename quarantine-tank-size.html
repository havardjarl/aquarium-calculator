Quarantine Tank Size for New Fish

A dedicated quarantine (QT) tank lets you watch new fish for disease before they join the display. A common starter QT is 10-20 US gallons with a heater, gentle filter, and bare bottom for easy cleaning.

Sizing rules of thumb

FishTypical QT
Nano / small community10 gal
Medium community / few adults20 long
Large or aggressive speciesMatch adult needs; do not undersize

How long

Many hobbyists quarantine about 2-4 weeks while watching for ich, velvet, and bacterial issues. Keep ammonia and nitrite at 0 with a cycled sponge filter or bottled bacteria plus testing.

Calculate QT volume with the aquarium calculator. For new setups, complete a fishless cycle or move established media from a healthy tank.
