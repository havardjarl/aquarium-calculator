Water Change Calculator & Guide

Dilution

Each change lowers dissolved nitrate by roughly the same percentage (if the new water has none). A 25% change takes 40 ppm to about 30 ppm; two 25% changes reach about 22.5 ppm.

Tips

Use a dechlorinator dosed for the full tank volume when using tap water with chloramine, and match the temperature.
