Aquarium Glass Thickness Calculator

This uses the plate-stress formula widely used for DIY aquarium glass: thickness = square root of (beta x water pressure x height squared / allowable stress). Beta depends on the length-to-height ratio of the panel, and the allowable stress assumes float glass with a modulus of rupture of about 19.2 N/mm2 and a safety factor of 3.8. Water height matters far more than length, because pressure grows with depth.

Important caveats

  • This is an estimate for ordinary float glass with a full bottom support, not engineering advice. Large tanks are often built with braces or thicker glass than the minimum.
  • Tempered glass cannot be drilled after tempering, and acrylic follows different rules.
  • The bottom pane usually needs to be at least as thick as the sides.

A tank this size is heavy when full; check the tank weight chart and the stand weight guide, and confirm volume with the aquarium calculator.
