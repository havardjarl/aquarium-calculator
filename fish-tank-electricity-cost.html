How Much Electricity Does a Fish Tank Use?

How the estimate works

Energy (kWh) = watts x hours / 1000. Filters and air pumps usually run around the clock. Lights run for the photoperiod you set. A heater only draws power while its thermostat is on, so the share of the day it runs depends on how much warmer the tank is than the room, the lid, and the tank size.

Ways to cut the cost

  • Use a lid or cover glass to reduce heat loss and evaporation.
  • Keep the tank away from drafts, windows and exterior walls.
  • Switch older fluorescent fixtures to LED.
  • Size the heater sensibly with the heater guide; an oversized heater does not use more energy on its own, but a room that is much colder than the tank does.

Check your own electricity bill for the real price per kWh; the default here is only an example value.
