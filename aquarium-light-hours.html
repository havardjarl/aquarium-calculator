How Many Hours Should Aquarium Lights Be On?

Common hobby guidance is about 6-8 hours a day for most fish-only and planted tanks, and up to around 10 hours for well-balanced planted setups. Fish do not need light at night, and a regular day-night cycle is generally recommended.

Typical photoperiods

SetupHours per day (rule of thumb)
Fish-only6-8
Low-tech planted6-8
High-light planted with CO27-10
New tank or algae outbreakStart at ~6 and increase slowly

Algae and timers

  • Too many hours of strong light is a common cause of algae; shorten the photoperiod before reaching for chemicals.
  • A simple outlet timer keeps the schedule consistent.
  • Some keepers split the day with a mid-day break (a "siesta"); results are mixed, so treat it as optional.
