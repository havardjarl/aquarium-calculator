What Size Tank Do Zebra Danios Need?

Zebra danios are small (about 2 in) but very active schooling fish. A common recommendation is a school of at least 6 in a tank of at least 10 US gallons, and many keepers prefer a 20 gallon long because danios use the full length of the tank to swim.

School size by tank (rule of thumb)

TankDanios, if they are the main fish
10 gal~6
20 gal long~8-12
29-30 gal~12-15

A bigger school usually spreads out chasing, which can otherwise be aimed at slow, long-finned tank mates.

Care notes

  • Danios tolerate a fairly wide range and are commonly kept around 65-77 F (18-25 C), so some keepers run them unheated in a warm room. Check the temperature guide.
  • They are fast jumpers, so a lid helps.
  • Gentle current is fine; size filtration with the filter flow guide.
