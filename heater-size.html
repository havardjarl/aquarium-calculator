What Size Aquarium Heater Do You Need?

A common rule of thumb is 3–5 watts per US gallon (roughly 1 W per liter). Use the high end when the room is much colder than the target temperature.

Rule by temperature difference

Target minus room tempWatts per gallon (rule of thumb)
Up to ~5 °F (3 °C)~2.5–3
~10 °F (5–6 °C)~3–4
~15 °F+ (8 °C+)~5

Two heaters for large tanks

For tanks above about 50–75 gallons many keepers split the wattage across two heaters at opposite ends: better heat distribution, and if one sticks on it is less likely to overheat the tank. A separate temperature controller adds another safety layer.

Always follow the heater manufacturer's rated tank size.
