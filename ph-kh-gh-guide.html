pH, KH and GH in Aquariums

pH measures how acidic or alkaline the water is. KH (carbonate hardness) buffers pH so it does not swing. GH (general hardness) is mainly dissolved calcium and magnesium.

Why KH matters

Low KH lets pH crash overnight in a planted or high-bioload tank. Many community fish tolerate a range of pH if KH is stable. Chasing an exact pH with chemicals often causes more harm than leaving a stable, reasonable value.

Practical tips

  • Test with a liquid kit; strips are rough.
  • Match new fish to your water when you can, instead of constantly remaking RO blends.
  • Driftwood and CO2 tend to lower pH; crushed coral and limestone raise KH/GH.

Hardness does not replace cycling: still keep ammonia and nitrite at 0. See the cycling guide and water change calculator.
