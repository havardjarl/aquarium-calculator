What Size Tank Does a Pleco Need?

"Pleco" covers many species with very different adult sizes. The bristlenose pleco usually stays around 4-5 inches and is commonly kept in tanks from about 20-30 US gallons. The common pleco sold as a small juvenile can grow to around 12-18 inches or more and often needs 125 gallons or more, or a pond.

Rough sizes by type

TypeAdult length (approx.)Common minimum tank
Bristlenose4-5 in~20-30 gal
Clown pleco3.5-4 in~20-30 gal
Rubber lip5-7 in~30-40 gal
Common / sailfin12-18+ in~125 gal or more

Sizes vary by source and individual; check the species before buying.

Care notes

  • Plecos produce a lot of waste, so size filtration generously (see the filter flow rate guide).
  • Many species rasp on driftwood, and some keepers consider it an important part of their diet.
  • Feed sinking wafers and vegetables rather than relying on algae alone.

A tank big enough for a common pleco is heavy; check the tank weight chart.
