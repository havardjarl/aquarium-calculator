What Size Tank Do Corydoras Need?

Corydoras are small, peaceful bottom dwellers that live in groups. A common recommendation is a group of at least 6 of the same species in a tank of at least 20 US gallons, ideally a 20 long (30 x 12 in) because they use the bottom, not the height. Pygmy and dwarf species can work in smaller tanks.

Substrate

Corys sift through the bottom for food. Many keepers prefer smooth sand or fine, rounded gravel so their barbels are not damaged. Work out how much to buy with the substrate guide.

Care notes

  • Many common species are kept around 72-78 F (22-26 C); check your species.
  • Use sinking food so it reaches them (see how much to feed fish).
  • They are often reported as sensitive to salt and some medications, so check labels before treating (see the salt guide).
