What Size Tank Do Guppies Need?

Adult guppies are small (about 1.5-2.5 in), but they are active and breed quickly. A common recommendation is a 10 gallon tank as a minimum for a small group, with 20 gallons giving far more stability. The calculator uses a cautious rule of roughly one guppy per 2 gallons as an upper bound, and fewer if you want room for fry.

Male to female ratio

Males chase females constantly. Keepers often suggest one male to two or three females, or an all-male tank if you do not want fry.

Water and care

  • Guppies are commonly kept around 72-82 F (22-28 C).
  • They tolerate a fairly wide pH range and many do well in moderately hard water (see the pH, KH and GH guide).
  • A mixed-sex group can multiply quickly, so plan for fry or rehoming.
