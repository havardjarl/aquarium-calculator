How Big a Tank Does a Betta Need?

A healthy adult betta does best in at least 5 US gallons (about 19 L). Many keepers prefer 10 gallons for stable temperature and water quality. Bowls and tiny cups swing temperature and ammonia too fast.

Why gallons matter for bettas

Bettas are labyrinth fish and can breathe air, but they still need space to swim, a heater for tropical temperatures (about 76-80 F / 24-27 C), and a gentle filter. Small volumes crash after a missed water change.
