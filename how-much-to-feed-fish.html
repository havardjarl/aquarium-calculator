How Much Should You Feed Your Fish?

A common rule is to feed only what your fish finish in about 1-2 minutes, once or twice a day. Most aquarium problems with feeding come from giving too much, not too little.

Why overfeeding is a problem

Uneaten food breaks down into ammonia, which feeds algae and can harm fish. If you see food on the substrate after a few minutes, feed less. Check what an ammonia reading means with the ammonia calculator.

Typical schedules (rule of thumb)

FishCommon schedule
Most adult community fishOnce or twice a day, small portions
Fry and juvenilesSeveral small feedings a day
Large predatory fish (e.g. oscars)Often once a day or every other day as adults
Bottom feedersSinking food so it reaches them

Fasting and vacations

  • Many keepers skip feeding one day a week for adult fish.
  • Healthy adult fish can usually go a few days without food; for longer trips, an automatic feeder you have tested beforehand is a common choice.
  • Avoid dropping in large "vacation blocks" in small tanks, as they can cloud the water.

Overfeeding also raises nitrate between water changes; plan changes with the water change calculator.
