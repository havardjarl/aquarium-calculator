What Size Tank Do Neon Tetras Need?

Neon tetras are small (about 1.5 in) schooling fish. A common recommendation is a school of at least 6, and ideally 8-10 or more, in a tank of at least 10 US gallons. A 20 gallon tank makes a larger school easier and keeps water conditions more stable.

School size by tank (rule of thumb)

TankNeons, if they are the main fish
10 gal~6-8
20 gal~10-15
29-30 gal~15-20

Reduce these numbers if you keep other fish in the same tank.

Water and care

  • Neons are commonly kept around 70-81 F (21-27 C).
  • Many keepers report they do best in soft, slightly acidic water; see the pH, KH and GH guide.
  • They are often described as sensitive to new tanks, so add them only after the tank is fully cycled (see the cycling guide).
  • Quarantine new fish first (see the quarantine tank guide).
