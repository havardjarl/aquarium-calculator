How to Calculate Aquarium Volume

Volume is the space inside the glass. Always use inside dimensions.

Rectangular

L × W × H (inches) ÷ 231 = US gallons. Example: 24 × 12 × 16 in = 4,608 in³ ÷ 231 ≈ 19.9 gal (≈ 75.5 L). In cm: L × W × H ÷ 1000 = liters.

Cylinder

π × r² × H. A 12 in diameter, 24 in tall cylinder: 3.1416 × 36 × 24 ≈ 2,714 in³ ≈ 11.8 gal.

Bow-front

Treat it as a rectangle plus a half ellipse: area ≈ L × D_back + (π/4) × L × (D_front − D_back), where D_back is the depth at the ends and D_front the depth at the center of the bow. Multiply by H, then ÷ 231.

Hexagon

Area = 2.598 × side² (regular hexagon), then × H.

Units

1 US gallon = 3.785 L = 231 in³. 1 UK (imperial) gallon = 4.546 L. Many UK and European tank sizes are quoted in liters.
