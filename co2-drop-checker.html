CO2 Drop Checker Colors & Targets

A drop checker with 4 dKH indicator solution is a common way to eyeball dissolved CO2 in planted tanks. Color is a rough guide, not a lab reading.

Color (typical)Rough meaning
BlueLow CO2
GreenOften ~30 ppm target zone
YellowHigh CO2; risk to fish

Practical tips

  • Aim for green during the photoperiod; adjust injection slowly over days.
  • Watch fish behavior (gasping at surface) more than the checker alone.
  • Pair CO2 with enough light and fertilizer; CO2 alone will not fix algae.

Lighting targets are in the LED lighting guide. For tank volume when dosing, use the aquarium calculator.
