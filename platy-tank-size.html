What Size Tank Do Platies Need?

Platies are small, hardy livebearers that usually reach about 2-3 inches. A common recommendation is a 10 US gallon tank as a minimum for a small group, with 20 gallons or more giving room for the fry that almost always follow in a mixed-sex group.

Rule-of-thumb stocking

TankPlaties, if they are the main fish
10 gal~3-5
20 gal~6-8
29-30 gal~8-12

Reduce these numbers when you keep other fish or plan to raise fry.

Care notes

  • Keep about one male to two or three females so no single female gets chased constantly.
  • Platies are commonly kept around 70-80 F (21-27 C) and generally prefer moderately hard, slightly alkaline water (see the hardness converter).
  • Add them only to a fully cycled tank (see the cycling guide).
