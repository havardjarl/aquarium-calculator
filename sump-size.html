How Big Should Your Sump Be?

A common guideline is a sump of roughly 20-30% of the display tank volume, with bigger usually better as long as it fits in the stand. The calculator above uses that range.

Plan for drain-back

When the return pump stops, water from the display drains down until it falls below the overflow, and return lines can siphon back. The sump must have enough empty space to hold that water without overflowing. Test it by switching the pump off with the sump at its normal running level.

Drain-back volume estimate

Drain-back from the display = water height above the overflow teeth (in) x display length x width (in) / 231 for US gallons. Add the volume of the plumbing that can siphon back.

Return pump flow

Many keepers aim for a sump turnover of around 3-10 times the display volume per hour, depending on the setup. See the filter flow rate guide and check the stand load with the stand weight guide.
