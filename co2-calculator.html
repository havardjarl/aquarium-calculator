Aquarium CO2 Calculator (pH and KH)

The formula

The common hobby estimate is CO2 (ppm) = 3 x KH (dKH) x 10^(7 - pH). Example: KH 4 and pH 6.8 gives 3 x 4 x 1.58, or about 19 ppm. Many planted tank keepers aim for around 30 ppm during the light period.

Why it can be wrong

The formula assumes carbonate is the only buffer in the water. Driftwood tannins, aquasoils, phosphate buffers and pH-adjusting products all throw it off, sometimes by a lot. That is why many keepers prefer a drop checker or measure the pH drop from fully degassed water to lights-on.

Safety

Fish gasping at the surface is a sign to reduce CO2 and increase surface agitation right away. Convert kit readings with the water hardness converter and match light levels with the LED lighting guide.
