Ammonia After a Water Change

Emergency dilution

Ammonia and nitrite should stay near 0 in a cycled tank. If you measure a spike, a large water change (often 50%+) with dechlorinated, temperature-matched water is the usual first step while you find the cause (overfeeding, filter crash, uncycled tank).

This tool only models dilution. It does not replace a liquid test kit or manufacturer guidance. Pair it with the fishless cycling guide and nitrate calculator.
