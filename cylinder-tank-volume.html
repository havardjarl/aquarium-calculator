Cylinder Tank Volume Calculator

Formula

Volume = pi x radius squared x height. In inches, divide cubic inches by 231 for US gallons. In centimeters, divide cubic cm by 1000 for liters. Example: a 12 in diameter tank filled to 24 in holds about 11.8 US gallons.

Half-cylinder and other shapes

For a half-cylinder (flat back), halve the result. Rectangular, bow-front and hexagon formulas are in the tank volume guide, and the main aquarium calculator handles rectangular tanks.
