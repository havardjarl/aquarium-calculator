Aquarium Surface Area Calculator

For a rectangular tank, surface area is simply the inside length x width. Height does not change it, which is why a long, shallow tank has more surface than a tall tank of the same volume.

Why surface area matters

Oxygen enters and carbon dioxide leaves the water at the surface. Two tanks with the same gallons can support different amounts of fish if one has much more surface. Surface agitation from a filter outlet or air stone increases gas exchange further.

The old surface area rule

An older hobby rule suggests about 1 inch of fish per 12 square inches of surface for tropical fish, and far less for coldwater fish such as goldfish. Like the inch-per-gallon rule, it ignores adult size, body shape and filtration, so treat it as a rough check only. The stocking guide covers better approaches.

Other shapes: see the tank volume guide and the cylinder tank calculator (a cylinder's surface is pi x radius squared).
