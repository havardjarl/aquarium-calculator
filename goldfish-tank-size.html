How Big a Tank Do Goldfish Need?

Fancy goldfish usually need at least 20 US gallons for the first fish and about 10 more gallons for each extra fancy. Common and comet goldfish get much larger and often need 40-75+ gallons each as adults, ideally in a pond or very large tank.

Why goldfish need more space

They produce a heavy bioload, grow larger than store juveniles suggest, and foul small bowls quickly. A filtered, cycled tank with frequent water changes matters more than decorations.
