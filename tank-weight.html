How Much Does a Full Aquarium Weigh?

Fresh water weighs about 8.34 lb per US gallon (1 kg per liter); saltwater about 8.5–8.6 lb/gal. Add glass, substrate, rock and stand.

A rough planning figure people use for the total setup is ~10 lb per gallon or more. For large tanks on upper floors, consult someone qualified about your floor; this site cannot assess structural loads.
