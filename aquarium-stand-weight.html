Aquarium Stands and Floor Load

Buy a stand rated for your filled weight, not the empty glass rating. Water alone is about 8.34 lb per US gallon; substrate, rock and the tank add more.

Checklist

  • Level the stand; shims beat a rocking cabinet.
  • Spread load on joists when you can; avoid mid-span on long tanks when unsure.
  • Upper floors and older homes may need a qualified opinion for large tanks.

Estimate water weight with the filled weight chart and real gallons from the calculator. This page is planning guidance only, not structural engineering advice.
