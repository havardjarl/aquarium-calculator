Aquarium Evaporation and Top-Off

Top-off volume = water level drop x length x width (inches) / 231 for US gallons. Measure the drop from your usual fill line.

Top off with fresh water

Only water evaporates; dissolved minerals and salt stay behind. That is why reef and saltwater tanks are topped off with fresh RO/DI water, not saltwater. In freshwater tanks, dechlorinate tap top-off water (see the dechlorinator dose calculator).

Why evaporation varies

Open tops, warm water, strong surface agitation and dry room air all increase evaporation. A lid cuts it noticeably and also saves heater energy (see the electricity cost calculator).
