Aquarium Filter Flow Rate: How Many GPH?

Common guidance: the filter's rated flow should turn over the tank volume about 4–6 times per hour for most freshwater community tanks, more for messy fish (goldfish, cichlids) and less for slow-water species like bettas.

Example: a 40 gallon tank × 5 = ~200 GPH rated flow.

Rated vs real flow

Manufacturer figures are usually measured without media or head height; real flow is lower once the filter is loaded. That is why many keepers size up.
