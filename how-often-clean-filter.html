How Often Should You Clean Your Filter?

Rinse mechanical media when flow drops or every few weeks, in old tank water, not tap water. Beneficial bacteria live on media; hot or chlorinated water can crash the cycle.

Rules of thumb

  • Never replace all media at once in a cycled tank.
  • Stagger sponge, floss and ceramic cleanings.
  • If ammonia or nitrite appear after a deep clean, do water changes and avoid more media swaps.

Size flow with the GPH guide. If you reset the filter on a new setup, follow the fishless cycling guide.
