How Many Fish for Your Aquarium?

The old one inch of fish per gallon rule is a rough starting point for slender freshwater community fish in a filtered, cycled tank. It fails for bulky fish (goldfish, cichlids), messy eaters, and tiny nano tanks where surface area and filtration matter more than gallons alone.

Better heuristics

  • Match adult size, not store juvenile size.
  • Prioritize footprint and swimming length for active species.
  • Stock slowly after a full fishless cycle; add a few fish every 1-2 weeks while testing ammonia and nitrite.
  • Overfilter for goldfish and large cichlids; under-stock for bettas and labyrinth fish that prefer calm water.
