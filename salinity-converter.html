Salinity: PPT to Specific Gravity Converter

This uses a linear approximation (SG of about 1 + 0.000754 x ppt) that is close for normal aquarium salinities at around 77 F (25 C). It is a quick estimate, not a laboratory conversion; temperature-compensated refractometers and meters are more precise.

Keep it stable

Evaporation raises salinity because salt stays behind, so top off with fresh RO/DI water. Estimate top-off volume with the evaporation calculator and freshwater salt dosing with the aquarium salt calculator.
