How Big a Tank Do Shrimp Need?

Dwarf shrimp such as cherry shrimp are often kept in tanks from about 5 US gallons upward. Larger tanks are more stable, which matters more for shrimp than for many fish. A common hobby rule of thumb is up to roughly 5-10 dwarf shrimp per gallon in a mature, planted tank, starting with a smaller group and letting them breed.

Setup tips

  • Use a sponge filter or cover the filter intake so baby shrimp are not pulled in.
  • Add shrimp only to a fully cycled tank; mature tanks with biofilm do best.
  • Avoid copper-based medications and fertilizers, which are commonly reported as harmful to invertebrates.
  • Keep temperature and hardness stable; read the pH, KH and GH guide.

Tank mates

Many fish will eat shrimp, especially babies. Shrimp-only tanks or very small, peaceful tank mates give the best results.
