What Size Tank Does an Oscar Need?

Oscars grow fast and commonly reach around 10-12 inches as adults. A widely cited hobby minimum is about 75 US gallons for one adult oscar, with roughly 125 gallons or more for a pair. A long footprint (at least about 18 in front to back) gives them room to turn.

Rule-of-thumb sizes

SetupCommon minimum
One juvenile (under 4 in)~40 gal, temporary only
One adult~75 gal
A pair~125 gal
Oscar plus large tank mates~150 gal or more

Filtration and maintenance

  • Oscars are messy eaters, so many keepers run filtration rated for well over the tank volume (see the filter flow rate guide).
  • Large weekly water changes help keep nitrate down; check yours with the nitrate calculator.
  • Keep a secure lid, since oscars can jump.
  • They are commonly kept around 74-80 F (23-27 C); size a heater with the heater calculator.

A full 75 gallon tank is heavy. Check the load with the tank weight chart and the stand weight guide.
