How to Cycle a New Aquarium (Fishless)

Cycling grows bacteria that convert toxic ammonia to nitrite and then nitrate. It commonly takes several weeks; there is no fixed timeline.

  1. Set up the tank, filter and heater; dechlorinate the water.
  2. Add an ammonia source (pure bottled ammonia or fish food) to about 2 ppm.
  3. Test ammonia, nitrite and nitrate every few days with a liquid test kit.
  4. Ammonia falls, nitrite rises, then nitrite falls and nitrate rises.
  5. It is cycled when 2 ppm ammonia clears to 0 ammonia and 0 nitrite within 24 hours. Do a large water change, then add fish gradually.
