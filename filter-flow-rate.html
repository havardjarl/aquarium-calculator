Aquarium Filter Flow Rate: How Many GPH?

Common guidance: the filter's rated flow should turn over the tank volume about 4–6 times per hour for most freshwater community tanks, more (often 6–10x) for messy fish such as goldfish and cichlids, and less for slow-water species like bettas.

Example: a 40 gallon tank × 5 = ~200 GPH rated flow.

Rated vs real flow

Manufacturer figures are usually measured without media or head height; real flow is lower once the filter is loaded and as media clogs. That is why many keepers size up rather than down.

When flow is too strong

Fish struggling to swim, hiding behind decor or being pushed around the tank are signs of too much current. Baffle the outlet with a sponge, aim it at the glass, or use a spray bar instead of buying a smaller filter. Bettas and long-finned fish are the usual cases (see the betta tank guide).

Keep the flow up

Flow drops as sponges clog. Rinse mechanical media in old tank water when flow falls (see how often to clean a filter). Use real water volume from the aquarium calculator, and for sumps see the sump size guide.
