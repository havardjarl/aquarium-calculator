How Often Should You Change Aquarium Water?

A common starting schedule for a stocked, filtered freshwater tank is a 20-30% water change once a week. Heavily stocked, messy, or new tanks often need larger or more frequent changes. Lightly stocked planted tanks sometimes go longer between changes if nitrate stays low.

Starting schedules

SituationCommon starting point
Typical community tank20-30% weekly
Goldfish / turtles / heavy bioload30-50% weekly or twice weekly
New tank still settlingTest daily; change if ammonia or nitrite rises
Planted, lightly stockedOften 20-30% weekly still, or as nitrate climbs

Let tests drive the schedule

If nitrate climbs above your target between changes, increase frequency or percentage. Use the nitrate calculator and the water change volume tool to plan each swap. Match temperature and dose dechlorinator for the full tank when using chloramine (see dechlorinator dose).

During a fishless cycle, follow the cycling guide rather than a fixed weekly habit.
