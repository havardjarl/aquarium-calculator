What Size Tank Does a Turtle Need?

A widely repeated hobby rule for aquatic turtles is about 10 US gallons of tank per inch of shell length for one turtle, with more space for each extra turtle. Sliders and cooters grow large, so many keepers plan for the adult size rather than the hatchling.

Typical adult sizes (approximate)

SpeciesAdult shellRule-of-thumb tank
Red-eared slider (male)~7-9 in~75-90 gal
Red-eared slider (female)~10-12 in~100-125 gal or a pond
Musk / mud turtle~4-5 in~40-50 gal
Painted turtle~5-7 in~55-75 gal

Sizes vary by individual and source; check a species-specific care guide.

Setup tips

  • Provide a dry basking area with a heat lamp and UVB lighting.
  • Turtles are messy; many keepers use a filter rated for two to three times the water volume.
  • Water depth of at least about 1.5 to 2 times the shell length lets them turn over safely.
