What Size Tank Does an Axolotl Need?

A common hobby recommendation is a 20 gallon long tank (30 x 12 in footprint) as a minimum for one adult axolotl, with roughly another 10 gallons for each additional axolotl. Footprint matters more than height because axolotls live on the bottom.

Temperature

Axolotls are coldwater animals. They are commonly kept around 60-64 F (16-18 C), and many sources warn that sustained temperatures above about 72 F (22 C) cause stress. They generally do not need a heater; in warm rooms keepers use fans or chillers. Convert units with the temperature guide.

Substrate and flow

  • Use bare bottom or fine sand; gravel small enough to swallow is a commonly cited impaction risk.
  • Axolotls prefer gentle flow, so a sponge filter or a baffled outlet is popular.
  • Cycle the tank fully first (see the fishless cycling guide).
