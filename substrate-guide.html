How Much Substrate Does Your Aquarium Need?

Substrate depends on the footprint (length × width), not gallons. A tall 29 gal and a 20 gal long can need similar amounts.

Formula

L × W × depth (in) = in³. Divide by 1,728 for cubic feet. Typical gravel and sand weigh roughly 90–110 lb per cubic foot (approximate bulk density; check the bag). Lightweight planted aquasoils are much lighter, often around half that.
