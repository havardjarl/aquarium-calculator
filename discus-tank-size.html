What Size Tank Do Discus Need?

Adult discus are large, tall fish. A common hobby recommendation is about 50-55 US gallons as a minimum for a small group, with many keepers preferring 75 gallons or more so the group can school without constant stress. Height helps: tanks around 18-24 inches tall are popular.

Rough sizes

SetupCommon minimum
Pair~40-55 gal, tall
Group of 5-6~55-75 gal
Larger group~90-125 gal

Discus often do better in a dedicated group than mixed with fin-nippers.

Care notes

  • They are commonly kept warmer than many community fish, often around 82-86 F (28-30 C); size the heater with the heater guide.
  • Soft, clean water and frequent water changes are widely recommended; plan changes with the water change calculator.
  • Confirm real volume with the aquarium calculator.
