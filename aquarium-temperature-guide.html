What Temperature Should a Fish Tank Be?

Most tropical community fish are kept around 75-80 F (24-27 C). Fancy goldfish and other coldwater species are commonly kept cooler, often in the mid 60s to low 70s F (about 18-22 C), without a heater in a normally heated home. Check the needs of each species you keep.

Stability matters most

Sudden swings stress fish more than a degree or two off target. Match the temperature of new water during water changes and use a separate thermometer to check the heater's dial. See the heater size guide and the betta tank guide.
