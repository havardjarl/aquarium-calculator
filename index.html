FAQ

How do I calculate aquarium gallons?

Multiply inside length × width × height in inches and divide by 231 to get US gallons. In centimeters, divide by 1000 to get liters.

Why is my actual water volume less than the label?

Labels are usually nominal. Glass thickness, substrate, decor and the gap below the rim typically reduce the real water volume by roughly 10–20%.
